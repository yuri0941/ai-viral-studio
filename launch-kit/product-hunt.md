# Product Hunt Launch — AI Viral Studio

## Tagline
OMEGA AI runs your content machine: viral hooks, scripts, scheduling, and analytics in one co-pilot.

## Description
AI Viral Studio is an all-in-one AI co-pilot for creators, marketers, and agencies. It researches trends, writes hooks and scripts, schedules posts across platforms, and predicts the best time to publish — so you can focus on being creative while OMEGA handles the rest.

## Key Features
- 🤖 OMEGA AI Chat with voice input and chain-of-thought reasoning
- 🎬 AI Video placeholder for Reels/Shorts/TikTok scripts
- 📅 Visual content scheduler with auto-publishing
- 📊 Analytics dashboard and viral predictions
- 💰 Subscriptions, revenue share, and white-label for agencies
- 🧠 Neural memory graph and autonomous dev studio
- 🌐 i18n: Russian + English out of the box
