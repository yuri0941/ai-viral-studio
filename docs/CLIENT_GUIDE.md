# Client Guide — AI Viral Studio

## Getting Started

1. Register at https://aiviral-studio.ru
2. Complete onboarding: niche, audience, brand voice
3. Choose your plan

## Your First Post

1. Open **AI Chat** and describe your idea, or use a template.
2. OMEGA generates hooks and a full script.
3. Go to **Scheduler** and create a post.
4. Pick platform, date, time, and media.
5. Save as draft or publish now.

## Scheduler

- Visual calendar shows all planned posts
- Drag posts between dates
- AI suggests best posting time
- Auto-publish to connected platforms

## Analytics

- Views, CTR, engagement per post
- Best performing content types
- Audience growth trends

## Payment

- Pay by card, crypto, or invoice depending on plan
- Subscriptions renew monthly
- Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel anytime in **Settings → Billing**
